# range

## What it does

Rewrites the task's noun as verbs ("a deck" becomes "get busy people to believe a story and decide under uncertainty in 30 minutes"), assembles a **crew** of four to six fields that are best at those verbs, always including an adversary, and has each crew member *do* their job: their method, their check, their output. It also builds one small experiment with an instrument from outside the field, then merges what each member changed and hands the result to the outside judge.

## When to reach for it

The agent reaches for it when you want something exceptional or original, when the work feels stuck inside one discipline, or when you ask for a team, panel or red team. Type `/range` to force it.

## Common questions

**Isn't this just role-play?** The skill is built to avoid that. Crew members are there to bring methods, not voices: a short seller researches the case against you rather than talking like a trader.

**Does it need subagents?** No. With parallel agents it gives each crew member its own job; without them it works through the crew in turn.

## It's working if

- At least one field on the crew surprised you.
- The adversary found something real, and it was answered or listed as open.
- There is a working experiment with a tool you would not have reached for.
