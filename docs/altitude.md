# altitude

## What it does

Climbs from the task to the **goal behind the goal** ("this deck, so that leadership decides, so that money moves to the right places") and tests whether the requested task is the best road there. The higher roads it checks: decisions over documents, replacing a process built for reading data by hand, and standing ownership of a state instead of a stream of one-off fixes. It proposes a higher road only when it clearly serves the outcome better, and confirms before changing the deliverable.

## When to reach for it

The agent reaches for it on its own when you ask for an artifact that feeds a meeting or decision, when you keep making the same kind of request, or when you say "keep", "own" or "make sure". Type `/altitude` to force it.

## Common questions

**Will it ignore what I asked for?** No. It keeps your task unless a higher road clearly serves the outcome better, and it asks before switching.

**Does it fit creative work?** Less so. Creative work often has no outcome above it, and the skill says to keep the task there.

## It's working if

- You see the chain from task to outcome written out.
- Meeting artifacts come back organised around decisions, with the fact that would flip each one.
- Repeated requests turn into a proposal for standing ownership with a list of what is needed.
